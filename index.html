Critical-thinking questions

  1. Who gains and loses when a tariff raises the domestic EV price?
  2. Why might tariff revenue initially increase but eventually fall to zero?
  3. How might a tariff affect domestic innovation and competitiveness over the next decade?
  4. What assumptions of the small-country model are questionable for the U.S.–China EV market?
  5. What evidence would help determine whether protecting domestic EV production is worth the costs?
